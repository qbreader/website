Teammates play separately, so their buzzes are merged afterwards: on each tossup only the earliest buzz counts, and it locks out the rest of the team whether or not it was correct. Teammates who both answered after the audio ended buzzed at the same time, so a correct answer from either counts.

Every pair of teams in a division plays one simulated match. On each tossup the team that buzzed earlier answers first: a correct answer scores and locks out the other team, while a wrong one takes its penalty, if any, and leaves the tossup to the other team's answer. If both teams answered after the audio ended, they buzzed at the same time, and two correct answers split the points. The team with more points wins the match. Only members who have finished the packet count, so a team plays once one of its members finishes, and its results stay put while the rest of the division plays. Teams are ranked by the share of matches won (Pct, with a tie as half a win), then by Points.

Points, Correct and Tossups heard count every member, finished or not. Tossups heard counts every tossup at least one teammate has played, so a team's average is provisional until all of its members finish the packet.
