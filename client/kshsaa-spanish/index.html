Coverage, sources & grading

Full practice mixes newly generated combinations with the sentence bank. New combinations are finite grammatical variations, not live AI output. The sentence bank is a filtered collection of 10,000 Spanish–English pairs; these are sourced examples, not newly generated sentences.

The bank comes from the May 2018 ManyThings/Tatoeba collection. Source screening and automatic checks do not guarantee every translation is correct. Flag a questionable card to exclude it on this device. Equivalent natural English is accepted through self-grading; there is no automatic semantic judge.

Sentence-bank vocabulary matches use exact written content words. Inflected forms are not automatically assigned to a lemma. A word may have a different sense in a sentence than its deck gloss. Grammar tags for sourced questions are automatically assigned and approximate. Generated cards have explicit skill tags.

The 595 tournament prompts come from your historical sets. Their original keys are preserved after the answer; older wording restrictions are not a strict rubric.
